How it works

From the first plan to the finish line.

  1. 1

    Plan

    Add stops from the race guide, set cutoffs and fuel, assign your crew and pack kit by stop.

  2. 2

    Race day

    Race Control tells everyone when to leave, what to hand over and how the runner's doing against the plan.

  3. 3

    Debrief

    See where the time went, what got eaten and what to change, then take it into the next race.

Race day

Know when to leave, not just where to go.

Crew Control works out when your runner will reach each stop from their pace so far, then tells you when to set off, allowing for the drive and setting up.

  • Leave-by times, drive times and live ETAs for every stop
  • Cutoffs and the finish target, and how far ahead or behind you are
  • Parking notes, weather and who's on the crew at each stop
  • A Live Activity keeps the next stop on your lock screen

Pit stops

Nail every pit stop.

When your runner arrives, one tap starts the clock. Everything you need is on one screen: what to hand over, how long you've got and a quick check on how they're doing.

  • A stop timer against the target for that stop
  • A handover list to tick off as each item goes out
  • Tap only what's wrong (stomach, feet, cold, low mood) and it's logged for later

Your crew

Everyone at the right stop.

Send your crew one link and they land straight on your race. Then decide who covers which stop, share the brief and see who's confirmed and who's read it.

  • Invite your crew with a link
  • Assign crew to stops and see who's confirmed at a glance
  • The captain owns the plan and can hand the role over

Planning

One plan for the whole crew.

Build the plan once and everyone sees the same thing. Skip the typing: import the race guide as a PDF or photos, and Crew Control reads out the stations, cutoffs and crew access for you to check.

  • Import stops from the race guide, and the course from a GPX file
  • Cutoffs, distances, fuel for each leg and planned rests
  • A race-day-ready checklist, so nothing's left for the morning

Kit

Pack every bag by stop.

Know what goes in which bag, what's packed and what's still to buy. Your kit locker carries over to the next race.

Backyard ultras

Built for the bell.

Lap times, a countdown to the next bell and what goes out every lap, for races that loop until one runner's left.

Debrief

Better at every race.

Minutes against plan at each stop, average pit stop time and fuel taken against planned. Every race goes in your logbook.
